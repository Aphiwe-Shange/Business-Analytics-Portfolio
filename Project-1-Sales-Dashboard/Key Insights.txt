# Key Insights 

## Sales Performance

The West region generated the highest sales revenue, while the South region recorded the lowest revenue

## Profitability 

Technology products were the most profitable category, contributing the highest overall profit. 
The West region generated the highest profit among all regions.

 
## Sales Trends

Sales showed steady growth over time, with a significant increase between 2015 and 2017.

## Product Performance

Phones and Chairs generated the highest sales among all sub-categories, making them key revenue drivers.

## Recommendations

1. Expand marketing and sales in high-performing regions, particularly the West region. 

2. Investigate factors contributing to lower sales performance in the South region.

3. Continue investing in Technology products due to their strong profitability. 

4. Review the performance of lower-selling and lower-profit categories to identify improvement opportunities. 

5. Prioritize inventory planning for high-demand products such as Phones and Chair.
