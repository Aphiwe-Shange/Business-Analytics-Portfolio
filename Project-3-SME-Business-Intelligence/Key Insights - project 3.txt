# Key Insights

## Revenue Performance 

The business generated total revenue of approximately 2026 million from over 5 000 customer orders. 

## Category Performance 

Technology generated the highest revenue among all product categories, making it the strongest-performing category. Furniture generated the second-highest revenue, while Office Supplies generated the lowest revenue. 

## Regional performance

The West region contributed the highest revenue, followed by the East region. The South region generated the lowest revenue and may require additional attention to improve performance. 

## Sales Trend

Revenue declined slightly between 2015 and 2016 before experiencing significant growth in 2017. Sales remained relatively stable throughout 2018 

## Customer Segment Analysis

Consumer customers contributed the highest proportion of revenue, accounting for more than half of total sales. Corporate customers contributed the second-largest share, while Home Office customers contributed the lowest revenue. 

## Top Products

Several products consistently generated high sales revenue, indicating strong customer demand and making them key contributors to overall business performance. 

## Recommendations

1. Increase investment in the technology category due to its strong revenue contribution. 

2. Investigate opportunities to improve sales performance in the South region.

3. Develop targeted marketing strategies for Corporate and Home Office customers to increase their revenue contribution. 

4. Maintain adequate inventory levels for top-selling products to avoid stock shortages. 

5. Continue monitoring sales trends and customer segments to identify future growth opportunities.
