# Business Requirements

## Business Objective 

Reduce registration  processing time and improve student satisfacation.

## Stakeholders

- Students
- Administrative Staff
- Finance Departments
- Academic Departments

## Functional Requirements

1. Students must be able to submit applications online.
2. The system must automatically validate application information.
3. The system must verify fee payments automatically.
4. Students must receive automated status updates.

## Expected Benefits

- Faster processing times
- Improved communication
- Reduces manual effort
- Better student experience
