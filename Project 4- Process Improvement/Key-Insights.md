# Recommendations 

## Recommandation 1 

Implement an online registration portal 
Root cause addressed: physical queues, paper forms, and staff re-keying data that students already provided. 
Self-service document upload with format and completeness validation at the point of sumbission; pre-population of returning student details from the student infornation system; save and resume for students on poor connections; a mobile first, low-bandwidth interface. 
Note the equity risk of students without devices or data nedd on-campus kiosks and assisted-registation stations, or the portal shifts the burden rather than removing it. 

## Recommendation 2 

Automated payment verification 
Direct intergration with the payment gateway and bank feed so proof-of-payment matching happens by referrence number instead of my email attachement; automatic release of registration holds once minimum payment thresholds or a signed financial agreement are met; rules-based handling of bursary, NSFAS and third-party sponsor payments, which are usually wher the manual exceptions pile up. Flag that reconciliation exceptions still need a human queue- the aim is to reduce the exception volume and not pretend it it non-existent. 

## Recomendation 3

Real-time status tracking 
A student-facing tracker showing stage, current owner, what's outstanding and expected turnaround; the same view for staff so departments stop phoning/emailing each other for updates. This is what actaully kills the "inefficient communication between departments" problem- a shared, single source of truth replaces email threads. Add an internal dashboard showing volumes and ageing per stage so bottlenecks re visible while they are happening.
